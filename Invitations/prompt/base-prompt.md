# Base Prompt: Watercolor Conversion

The starting point for every invitation illustration. A per-topic variant (see `variants/`) extends this with hidden symbolic elements and a theme-specific palette/mood for a given Mishmar or event.

---

Transform the provided image into a vibrant watercolor painting in A4 portrait orientation. Reinterpret the original palette with moderate enhancements—boost saturation lightly to preserve true hues without drastic hue or tone shifts. Incorporate lively accent colors (teal, coral, golden yellow) alongside natural greens and warm earthy browns to introduce dynamic contrast. Render forms using a blend of wet-on-wet washes for softness and wet-on-dry strokes for crisp definition, ensuring fluid yet structured edges. Apply a high dynamic range lighting scheme: bright highlights, nuanced midtones, and gentle shadows that add depth without harshness. Maintain an overall light, airy, and full-of-life atmosphere that feels energetic but not overly dramatic.
